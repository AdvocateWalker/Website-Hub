Policy

Privacy Policy

Applies to all Advocate Network services, including Discord bots, ADV Core Bot, and connected Reddit moderation modules.

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

Scope

ADV processes only data needed for operations, moderation workflows, safety, and reliability.

Data categories

  • Platform IDs and configuration metadata
  • Operational logs and scheduler/runtime diagnostics
  • Moderation context required by enabled modules

Use and sharing

  • Used for requested features, security, and troubleshooting
  • No sale of personal data
  • Shared only for legal/security/platform compliance needs

Retention and controls

Data retention is limited to operational and safety needs. Admins/mods control feature scope and can disable services in their communities.
