Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to all Advocate Network (ADV) services in this ecosystem, including ADV Discord bots, ADV Core Bot, and connected Reddit automation modules and moderation tools.

Last Updated: May 24, 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers ADV services deployed across Discord and Reddit, including moderation automation, modmail tools, safety workflows, dashboards, and related scheduler or notification services.

2. Data We Collect

  • Platform identifiers (server, guild, subreddit, channel, thread, role, user, post, comment, conversation IDs)
  • Configuration and settings data (feature toggles, thresholds, policy overrides, authorized targets)
  • Operational metadata (job runs, command usage, module triggers, action outcomes)
  • Moderation context data needed for enabled features (mod log signals, modmail metadata, enforcement status)
  • Error, diagnostics, and anti-abuse telemetry

3. Message and Content Handling

ADV processes content only when required by enabled features. Examples include modmail processing, configured support channels, safety review pipelines, and explicit moderator workflows.

ADV does not use your data for advertising and does not sell personal data.

4. How Data Is Used

  • Operate requested bot features and moderation workflows
  • Provide safety controls, triage context, and policy enforcement routing
  • Maintain reliability, detect abuse, and troubleshoot failures
  • Generate audit trails for moderator-visible bot actions

5. Cross-Platform and Cross-Community Context

Some features may correlate signals across authorized communities or systems (for example, Reddit moderation context and Discord operator workflows). These features are intended for moderation use and should be configured by authorized admins/moderators only.

6. Legal Basis and Permissions

You are responsible for enabling ADV only where you have authority to do so. By using ADV, server owners and moderators represent they are authorized to process moderation-relevant data under applicable platform rules and local law.

7. Data Retention

Data is retained only as long as needed for operations, safety, abuse prevention, and auditability. Retention duration may vary by feature and incident response needs.

8. Data Sharing

ADV may share data only when required by law, platform policy compliance, security response, or to protect users and infrastructure. ADV does not broker or sell user data.

9. Security

ADV uses reasonable technical and organizational controls to protect stored data. No system is guaranteed to be perfectly secure, but access is limited to operational need.

10. Admin and Moderator Controls

  • Enable or disable features at the community level
  • Adjust policy settings and automation scope
  • Remove ADV services from a community
  • Request operational support through official ADV channels

11. Children and Age Requirements

ADV is not intended for anyone who is not permitted to use the underlying platforms (Discord/Reddit) under their Terms and age requirements.

12. Policy Updates

We may update this policy over time. Continued use of ADV services after updates means you accept the revised policy.

13. Contact

For privacy or data handling questions, contact ADV through official support channels listed by the project/community.

Summary

  • One policy for all ADV bots and automation services
  • Data use is limited to operations, moderation, and security
  • No sale of user data
  • Admins and moderators control feature scope
